A LITTLE CLARITY

Beauty with context.

Your photos and face landmarks stay in this browser tab. Removing a photo clears them. GlowSync sends only preferences you choose to the local server.

Product images may load from official brand or image hosting sites if a local copy is unavailable. These sites receive ordinary image requests, never your photo.

Product prices are dated quotes in INR. A saved quote does not guarantee current stock, discounts, delivery charges or availability. “Visit brand” opens the source website, where purchasing takes place.

Price watches work for connected feeds while you use the app. In-app alerts require a fresh, in-stock quote for the exact saved variant. There is no email or push delivery.

Photo tone suggestions and colour palettes are approximate styling aids. They do not identify people, diagnose skin, or guarantee a cosmetic shade match.
